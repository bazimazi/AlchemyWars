# Specification coverage

This maps every numbered section of the supplied specification to the implementation and its evidence. "Implemented baseline" means the system has working code, not that every illustrative example is a separate promised feature. The plan explicitly makes monetization/gacha optional and asks for content to grow after validation.

The software meets the numerical MVP content counts and has working implementations for the gaps identified after the TypeScript migration and the subsequent discovery/build review. This is implementation coverage, not proof of every qualitative goal or illustrative example. Automated verification does not establish human campaign pacing, physical-device 60 FPS, long-term enjoyment, retention or commercial service readiness.

## Storm chain and evidence review (0.11.0)

- **Reactions and research (8, 12, 13, 25, 30):** Storm Catalysis adds nine authored atmospheric recipes through the twentieth study, reusing existing elements. Actual research, Water/Lightning mastery three, Wet and storm conditions are enforced in experiments and combat. A developed formation produces ten successive recipes in a real Stormlands campaign encounter.
- **Chain counts and achievements (25, 80, 81):** reaction lengths count the causal recipe path independently of propagation depth. Permanent/daily allied counts exclude enemy chains and separate casts. The Ten Reaction Chain achievement has a verified route and once-only resource claims; saved facts survive event/history pruning, replay, concurrent online requests and restart.
- **Reports and codex (28, 63, 64, 65):** the longest allied trace retains source, time and ordered targets independently of clipped logs or frame capture. It reaches the codex even when the general chain log fills. Codex browsing groups prefixes and retains branches; each known step prepares its full lab requirements with keyboard focus.

Evidence: `test/chains.test.ts`, `test/server.test.ts`, `e2e/chains.spec.ts`; strict TypeScript, content validation and production build, 321 engine/server tests and 74 desktop/mobile browser checks. All 63 campaign encounters retain a winning route and browser play completes eight roguelite floors. New report steps, codex preparation and study cards were visually inspected at both viewports. The catalog is 137 recipes / 87 usable elements. The illustrative 500-discovery target still exceeds current content; human pacing, physical-device frame rate and hosted community operations require separate validation. Refreshed reports cover 5,600 campaign battles and 280 runs / 2,384 battles. Developed Storm now wins all 100 Tempest Titan seeds; loaned run policy outcomes are unchanged. Earlier review counts below describe those releases.

## Talent and laboratory review (0.10.0)

- **Permanent talents (50):** eighteen nodes across all six named branches with prerequisites, costs, branch navigation, completion counts and keyboard focus. Old node IDs remain compatible; imported orphan paths and invalid authored graphs are rejected.
- **Experiment slots and clues (13, 50, 65, 69):** a one-slot notebook expands to two through Parallel Notes. - **Economy and exploration (44, 49, 50):** shared rounded research/crafting discounts, once-only new-study essence, first-encounter knowledge and first-guardian shards. Reports display actual settled campaign rewards. Retrying or replaying cannot repeat first-clear bonuses.

Launched combat snapshots remain deterministic; normalized competition and loaned runs exclude account perks. Save/account restart repair preserves earned notebook capacity and valid prerequisite paths.

Evidence: `test/talents.test.ts`, `test/server.test.ts`, `e2e/talents.spec.ts`; strict TypeScript, content validation and production build, 301 engine/server tests and 68 desktop/mobile browser checks. All 63 campaign encounters retain winning routes; the eight-floor browser run remains covered. Talent branches, notebooks and field clues were visually inspected at both viewports. Refreshed reports cover 5,600 campaign battles and 280 runs / 2,384 battles. Account talent changes affect some developed formations; loaned run outcomes are unchanged. The new developed PvE chain ceiling is ten, but an earnable ten-link achievement still needs a verified authored route. The 500-discovery example remains beyond the 128-recipe catalog; human pacing, device frame rate and commercial operations require separate validation.

## Run experimentation review (0.8.0)

- **Runs and build choice (18, 21, 31, 47):** temporary relic/passive drafts, run-local equipment validation, targeting/casting priorities and ordered learned-recipe priorities. Every continuing victory offers recovery alongside reproducible build choices, including after collections are exhausted.
- **Conditional discoveries (13, 31, 65):** saved run target scenarios for statuses, health, shielding, enemy count and tags; the floor fixes the environment. Run experiments honor suppressed tags and eligible alternate rules. Account research/mastery cannot be forged, and discoveries remain temporary until settlement.
- **Endless rules (32, 56, 77):** deterministic floor mutations every three encounters, merged with Infinite Alchemy's captured expedition rule. Preparation explains current rules and transitions; launched snapshots and replays retain the original modifiers.
- **Persistence and evidence (62, 88, 110, 112):** old run migration, malformed collection repair, server protection during issued battles, restart/replay persistence and once-only retirement. A dedicated seeded run report and CI sample cover build drafts and changing rules.

Evidence: `test/runs.test.ts`, `test/server.test.ts`, `e2e/runs.spec.ts`, `scripts/run-balance.ts`; strict TypeScript, content validation and production build, 262 engine/server tests and 52 desktop/mobile browser checks. Verification retains winning routes through all 63 campaign encounters and an eight-floor browser run. Balance evidence includes 5,600 campaign matchups plus 280 runs / 2,384 battles with reload normalization. Run tools, conditional laboratory controls and mutation notes were visually reviewed. Fixed-policy results show build failures; they do not establish optimal play or human pacing.

Evidence: `test/guardians.test.ts`, `test/server.test.ts`, `e2e/guardians.spec.ts`; strict TypeScript, content validation and production build, 245 engine/server tests, 44 desktop/mobile browser checks and a refreshed 5,600-battle balance batch. This strengthens playable rules and presentation; human pacing and physical-device performance remain unverified.

## Implementation added after the audit (0.4.0)

- **Guild systems (41):** shared elemental imprints, four weekly missions and a hidden-reaction research project. Distinct experiment contributions cost knowledge, unlock a shared recipe at twenty contributions, and retain account-level weekly limits across guild changes. `server/guilds.ts`, `src/ui/community-expansion.ts`, server/browser tests.
- **PvP modes (38):** Element Wars and Weekly Crucible use weekly complementary pools, equal vessels, reproducible opponent seeds, separate standings and once-per-opponent weekly scoring. `src/core/competition.ts`, server/browser tests.
- **Codex and profile (27, 28, 80, 84, 85):** creature, artifact, lore and persistent combat-chain catalogs; collection relationship counts; highest campaign boss, total discoveries and PvP rating. Full mastery now correctly means level ten. `src/core/codex.ts`, `src/ui/codex-expansion.ts`, persistence/browser tests.
- **Unit architecture (17):** two configurable ability slots, four interpreted abilities with conditions/cooldowns, fifteen individual growth and animation profiles, and capped campaign-earned vessel levels. PvP excludes account growth. `src/data/units.ts`, `src/core/combat.ts`, engine/browser tests.
- **Live operations (90, 108, 121):** authenticated publication of validated content packs and bounded balance settings. Updates persist before activation, wait for issued battles to settle, synchronize to clients and survive offline reload. New rules require interpreted effect primitives; new executable mechanics still require an application release. `src/core/releases.ts`, `test/releases.test.ts`, browser tests.
- **Endgame and content depth (78, 109, 117):** daily challenges compose independent loans, restrictions, environments and enemies. Three Mythic elements and 57 authored continuation recipes expand the catalog to 124 reactions and 87 elements. All elements have outgoing recipes; multiple inputs/outputs and strategic value remain design goals rather than a claim that every node has equal connectivity. `src/data/continuations.ts`, `src/core/challenge-generator.ts`, graph and determinism tests.
- **Performance implementation (87):** bounded pooling reuses temporary trigger-event objects without retaining them in replay reports. The latest 0.11.0 5,600-battle benchmark reports median 9.02 ms and p95 19.43 ms simulation latency on this shared development machine; timings are not a controlled release comparison. This does not measure rendering FPS. `src/core/pool.ts`, `src/core/combat.ts`, `docs/BALANCE.md`.

**Validation still required:** human campaign pacing (5-10 hours), physical-device performance, assistive-technology testing, long-term balance, retention and enjoyment. These are separate from the implementation gaps above.

**Optional branches:** real-money cosmetics, premium currency and gacha remain intentionally omitted; the specification does not require enabling them.
