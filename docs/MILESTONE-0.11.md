# Storm catalysis and causal chain reports

Version 0.11.0 makes the specification's ten-reaction achievement earnable through an authored combat route. Storm Catalysis is the twentieth study, requiring Pressure Dynamics and Catalyst Architecture. Nine atmospheric recipes reuse existing outputs, bringing the catalog to 137 recipes and 87 usable elements. Actual Water and Lightning mastery level three, a Wet target and a storm are required. The laboratory honors account research/mastery rather than values supplied in an experiment scenario.

The verified sequence is:

1. Conductive: Water + Lightning -> Conductive.
2. Flooded Current: Conductive + Water -> Storm Surge.
3. Saturated Front: Storm Surge + Water -> Storm Cloud.
4. Charge Reservoir: Storm Cloud + Water -> Thunderstorm.
5. Ion Rain: Thunderstorm + Water -> Ionization.
6. Corona Discharge: Ionization + Water -> Plasma.
7. Flash Evaporation: Plasma + Water -> Steam.
8. Rapid Expansion: Steam + Water -> Blizzard.
9. Ice Spectrum: Blizzard + Water -> Aurora.
10. Liquid Lens: Aurora + Water -> Prism.

Wet supplies the catalytic Water affinity as residues are consumed. Each stage uses existing interpreted effects, trading direct damage for small shields, healing and short control effects. Adequate depth combines Chain Resonance, Reaction Scholar, Recursive Binding, Genesis Thread and Echo Catalyst; the talent prerequisite path includes Deep Binding and Parallel Notes. Genesis Thread requires fifty discoveries. Tests use owned, unlocked upgrades and a real Stormlands encounter with the nine new recipes initially unknown. The proof battle is a defeat: the achievement requires producing the chain, not defeating that opponent. A shallower formation or missing study cannot produce the full sequence.

Reaction counts now follow actual recipes in the causal path. Additional propagation steps still consume the depth/event budget, but cannot inflate a reaction count. The engine retains a separate allied maximum and its source, timestamp and ordered targets, independently of capped event logs or frame capture. Enemy chains and unrelated casts cannot earn the achievement. Permanent/daily facts survive codex/analytics pruning; old saves derive allied history only from validated saved chains, without trusting the legacy enemy-inclusive high score.

Reports explain each step and its conditions. The chain codex hides strict prefixes, preserves branches and lists longer sequences first. It records the longest trace even if the general chain log is exhausted. A known step prepares a complete laboratory scenario and moves keyboard focus to Combine. Research cards explain the new requirements, and gradually discovering all nine recipes in the lab does not award a combat-chain achievement. Achievement claims pay 200 gold and 25 knowledge once, including simultaneous server requests, replays and account restarts.

Verification: strict TypeScript, content validation and production build; 321 engine/server tests and 74 desktop/mobile browser checks. Winning routes remain verified through all 63 campaign encounters, and browser play completes eight roguelite floors. New browser checks cover real chain reports, replay/reload/claim persistence, codex preparation and gradual lab discoveries. Study cards and chain steps were visually inspected at both viewports. Offline assets include the new data/UI modules; the content version and cache advance while account saves remain compatible.

The refreshed campaign report covers 5,600 seeded battles: 58.70 seconds total, 9.02 ms median simulation and 19.43 ms p95 on this shared development machine. Developed Storm's Tempest Titan win rate changes from 0% to 100%, with draws changing from 8% to 0%; other win/draw rates retain prior values. Some Tempest matchups change duration/contribution/survivors. Timing is slower than the prior report and is not a controlled comparison or a rendering frame-rate measurement. The run report covers 280 runs / 2,384 battles in 6.28 seconds, with the same fixed-policy outcomes and a sixteen-floor ceiling. Runs omit account research/talents, so the new atmospheric cycle is unavailable there.

The illustrative 500-discovery achievement still exceeds the 137-recipe catalog. Human campaign duration, long-term enjoyment, assistive-technology accessibility, physical-device 60 FPS and operating a hosted multiplayer community remain separate validation/operational work. Optional payment and gacha branches remain omitted as permitted by the specification.
