# Milestone 0.6: research and tactical choice

The specification's research tree needs distinct branches and unlocks beyond stat modifiers and primordial elements. Combat also needs to evaluate every eligible recipe before honoring a player's reaction priorities.

Scope:

- Seven research branches, prerequisite-aware purchases and discounted prices.
- Six additional nodes unlocking four conditional recipes, a crafted catalyst, a vessel ability and a third PvE ability slot.
- Account research conditions derived from authoritative player state, with save and replay support. Competitive battles use two ability slots and omit account research bonuses.
- All eligible combat recipes considered, with explicit priorities before default priorities and fallback when another rule is disabled, guarded or on cooldown.
- Observatory objective, daily goal, claimable rewards and quick battle entry.
- Engine, authority and desktop/mobile browser verification, plus a seeded balance batch and updated coverage evidence.

Completed as version 0.6.0. Strict TypeScript, content validation, production build, 229 engine/server tests and 36 desktop/mobile browser tests pass. The 5,600-battle balance batch is recorded in BALANCE.md. Research cards, mobile formation tools and home objectives were visually reviewed. Known-recipe preparation also retains its instructions and restores frozen targets. Broader product goals still require human playtesting and deployment operations.
